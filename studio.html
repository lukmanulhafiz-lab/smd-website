The premise

Most good ideas never become products.

The distance between “we should build this” and “it’s live and people rely on it” is where projects stall — recruiting, agency overhead, cost, and time. Sehat Masa Depan exists to close that gap: a small, senior studio that turns a concept into a real, launched product, fast — and stays to help it grow.
