SERVICE — 03

Capacity Building

Training for healthcare professionals, physician-designed and physician-delivered. Clinical audiences notice immediately whether the person teaching them has done the work — so we do not outsource that part.

The problem this solves

  • Vendor training tends to teach the product, not the clinical reasoning around it — so adoption stalls the moment a case falls outside the demo.
  • Generic corporate training translated into a clinical setting loses credibility in the first ten minutes, and it does not recover.
  • Programmes are built around content to be covered rather than behaviour to be changed, and then measured by attendance.

What we do

  1. Curriculum and outcome designStarting from the decision or behaviour that should change, then working backwards to content — rather than starting from slides that already exist.
  2. Online course and MOOC developmentStructuring material for self-paced delivery at scale, including assessment design that tests judgement rather than recall.
  3. Data-driven decision-makingTraining clinical and programme staff to read, question and act on their own data — including where the data is weaker than it looks.
  4. Live deliveryWorkshops, webinars and in-person sessions for clinicians, clinic owners and programme teams, in Indonesian or English.

Who this is for

  • Health-tech companies whose product needs clinical users to change how they work
  • Hospital and clinic groups building internal capability
  • Health programmes that need field teams to use their own data
  • Professional bodies and training centres commissioning speakers on digital health and health regulation
On compliance-sensitive topics. Where training touches advertising, patient acquisition or professional conduct, the material is built directly on the governing regulation and quotes it in full — including the exceptions. Teaching a rule without its exception is how well-intentioned training creates the violation it was meant to prevent.

Have an audience and a problem?

Tell us who is in the room and what should be different afterwards. The programme design follows from that, and a first call is usually enough to tell whether a course, a workshop, or nothing at all is the right answer.
